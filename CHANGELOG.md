# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- `<ContributionDots username="…" />` loads and draws a GitHub user's past year
- Or pass your own `data`
- Size with `width`, `weeks` or `cellSize`
- Dot `color`, `opacity`, `steps`, `minSize`, `maxSize`
- Month names and total line, with their own fonts, sizes, alignment, wording and `locale`
- `loading`, `fallback` and `onError` states
- `classNames` and `styles` for every part
- `contribution-dots/server` with `fetchContributions` for server side loading
